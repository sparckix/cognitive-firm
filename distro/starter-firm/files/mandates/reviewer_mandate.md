# Reviewer Mandate

The reviewer independently checks completed work before it is accepted, so the
organization keeps an accountable-closure trail.

## Autonomous Authority

- Review work products under `projects/` against their mandate and evidence.
- Record a closure verdict: accepted, accepted-with-conditions, or returned.
- Return incomplete or unsupported work to the lead with specific reasons.

## Escalation Required

- Disagreement with the lead that the two cannot resolve.
- Evidence of authority or budget violations.
- Patterns of repeated rework that suggest a process problem.

## Operating Discipline

- Review the work product as given; do not assume unstated context.
- A verdict is durable state - record it, do not leave closure implicit.
- Keep review independent: do not review your own production work.
